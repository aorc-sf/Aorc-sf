EDUCATION & ORGANIZING

Knowledge becomes power when people can act together.

AORC's education work is not just about explaining policy. It is about building civic infrastructure: shared information, organized participation, measurable priorities, policy accountability, leadership development, and permanent community institutions.

Information Infrastructure

Translate legislation, budgets, hearings, programs, contracts, and implementation decisions into clear electronic updates.

Community Network

Use opt-in signups, surveys, the Community Board, opportunity alerts, and public education to organize people around shared interests.

Representation

Turn participation data, testimony, letters, meetings, and public comment into evidence of organized community priorities.

Accountability

Track appropriations, agency actions, contracts, grants, and results so announcements can be compared with what reaches communities.

Leadership

Create roles in outreach, research, education, organizing, partnerships, local boards, and future city chapters.

Institution Building

Convert advocacy into durable resources, services, ownership pathways, partnerships, and community infrastructure.

FLAGSHIP POLICY FRAMEWORK

The American Ownership & Reparative Capital Plan

AORC's broader policy work remains centered on repairing historical economic exclusion through ownership, productive assets, business formation, housing, investment, education, and long-term economic independence.

Restitution through ownership

Expand access to homes, businesses, investment capital, education and durable productive assets.

Build more housing

Pair purchasing power with construction, rehabilitation, community ownership and activation of underused housing.

Create more businesses

Turn capital into employers, suppliers, commercial ownership, jobs, tax revenue and stronger local economies.

Permanent capital

Explore independently governed trust structures designed to preserve and compound productive capital.

Fiscal discipline

Evaluate progressive financing, institutional restitution, verified savings, investment returns and safeguards.

Economic autonomy

Increase household power to survive shocks, make choices, invest, build and pass assets forward.

HELP BUILD THE RESOURCE CENTER

Public support petition

Petition statement

We call on public agencies, philanthropic institutions, universities, foundations, businesses, and community partners to support the creation of the American Ownership Resource Center as a public-interest hub for education, resource navigation, community organizing, reparative economic development, housing and business ownership strategy, descendant-focused policy work, and implementation accountability.

We support a proposed first-stage startup funding goal of $250,000–$500,000 to establish basic operating capacity, with funding subject to transparent budgeting, conflict-of-interest safeguards, public reporting, and measurable deliverables.
